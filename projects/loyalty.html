Brief

A direct-to-camera speaker explains the cost of uncertainty between an online purchase and the parcel reaching the customer. The edit adds emphasis with captions, a short on-screen list and cutaway delivery imagery.

Key decisions

The visual treatment prioritizes spoken clarity: captions follow the presenter’s words, information appears briefly as supporting graphics, and a delivery cutaway gives the abstract discussion a concrete example.

01 / FRAMEWORK

Hook

A direct-to-camera opening frames the post-purchase experience as the topic.

02 / FRAMEWORK

Evidence within the narrative

On-screen prompts name abandoned carts, cancellations and missed repeat purchases as risks discussed in the film; they are presented as its message, not independently measured results.

03 / FRAMEWORK

Resolution

The ending shifts to a doorstep delivery scene and the Shadowfax sign-off.
