FESTIVE FILM · MOTION / 07

A festive race to the doorstep.

A 30-second Christmas-themed brand film bringing a delivery rider and Santa into the same fast-moving story.

The work

The film begins with a familiar Christmas delivery scene: Santa and his reindeer flying into the city. A Shadowfax rider joins the action, leading into a playful race and a doorstep delivery moment that resolves the joke around speed.

The visual approach

Cinematic night scenes, quick changes of viewpoint and kinetic action turn an everyday delivery promise into a short festive narrative. The green-and-yellow rider acts as the recognisable brand anchor against the dark holiday setting.
