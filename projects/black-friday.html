SEASONAL CAMPAIGN · CAROUSEL / 03

Built for the busiest shopping days.

A five-frame Black Friday campaign presenting logistics capacity, speed, tracking and reliability as one connected promise.

← All work← Previous

Format

Disciplines shown

The work

The cover establishes the seasonal event, then each frame focuses on a different reason a commerce brand might need a dependable delivery network during peak demand. The storytelling is business-facing, but the visuals stay human: parcels, riders and a customer tracking a shipment.

The visual approach

The recurring “A network…” headline structure makes the campaign work as a series. Each frame pairs one sharply phrased message with a recognizable delivery situation, leaving the product value immediately legible even during a fast scroll.

01 / FRAMEWORK

Peak rush

The opening benefit centers on handling heavy parcel volume without slowing down.

02 / FRAMEWORK

Movement and visibility

A rider image communicates quick pickup and same-day delivery; a phone screen brings live tracking into view.

03 / FRAMEWORK

Close on confidence

The final frame returns to delivery staff and network infrastructure to underline reliability on high-demand days.
