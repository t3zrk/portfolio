The brief in the creative

Translate a technical, easily misunderstood cost issue into a shipping story that a small business can follow within the opening moments of a reel.

How the story unfolds

A presenter compares two parcel boxes: the smaller, heavier parcel and a larger, lighter one. On-screen “200gm” and “500gm” labels make the mismatch concrete. The film then cuts to parcel-weighing and warehouse imagery, using actual-weight and volumetric-weight graphics to explain why the bill can change. Shadowfax360 appears as the branded product framing for more predictable shipping costs.

The visual approach

The opening relies on a person, two physical boxes and a very short numerical comparison; those elements remain legible in a vertical feed. Captions follow the spoken explanation, while weight labels, rupee symbols and product-green interstitials organize the more technical section. The longer runtime allows the video to move from the everyday example to product details without overloading its first few seconds.
