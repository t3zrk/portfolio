GROWTH ANNOUNCEMENT · MOTION / 08

Make a network milestone move.

A vertical announcement film using a digital truck and animated numbers to visualise expansion in PIN-code reach.

The work

A point-cloud-style truck appears against a dark field. The video shifts into high-contrast numeric statements and brand-green typography, announcing a move from the earlier figure shown in the creative to 15,656 PIN codes.

The visual approach

The contrast between a detailed wireframe-like vehicle and spare typography makes the milestone the main event. The number is treated as the motion-led hero, not a footnote.
