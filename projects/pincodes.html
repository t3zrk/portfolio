Brief

The film opens behind a Shadowfax truck as its doors swing open and parcels spill toward the camera. An animated location marker and PIN-code numbers then transform the delivery scene into a direct statement about the network’s reach.

Key decisions

One visual metaphor does the work: the truck physically opens up to reveal reach. Strong dark-to-bright contrast and a rapid sequence are designed to make the numbers readable within a short vertical format.
