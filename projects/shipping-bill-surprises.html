The brief in the creative

Make a familiar, potentially complicated shipping-bill issue feel immediate enough for a short vertical scroll, then connect the concern to a specific product message.

How the story unfolds

A presenter addresses the viewer directly with a provocative line about the shipping bill. A fast transition changes the scene, and large on-screen labels name RTO and COD charges. The video closes on Shadowfax360’s proposed starting rate and “Zero COD Charges / No extra RTO Charges” messaging, framed as claims made within the original promotional creative.

Key decisions

A front-facing presenter, high-contrast green subtitle boxes and a fast room-to-room transition carry the message. Large yellow and red fee labels break up the talking-head format at the moment the costs are named; the concluding product message returns to a calmer framed shot with the Shadowfax mark.
