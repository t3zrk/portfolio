## Work archive — complete project copy

### Shadowfax360
**Format:** PRODUCT CAMPAIGN · CAROUSEL
**Hero:** A green light for growing businesses.
**Card copy:** A four-frame product carousel turning shipping capabilities into straightforward reasons for a small business to get moving.
**Project description:** The story begins with a green traffic signal and an invitation to start a business. Each following frame introduces a concrete part of the Shadowfax360 offer: nationwide PIN-code reach, D+2 COD remittance and a flat-rate delivery proposition.
**Visual approach:** The visual direction keeps the offer easy to scan: a single dominant image on each frame, high-contrast green headings and generous white space. The green signal becomes a simple visual shorthand for getting started.
**Sequence:**
- **The opening:** A traffic light leads the first frame, making “green light” both the campaign hook and the main visual.
- **The rollout:** A location pin, delivery handoff and logistics vehicle carry the progression from reach to payments to shipping costs.
- **The takeaway:** The final frame closes on a compact explanation of flat delivery rates, no weight slabs and no COD charges, as stated in the original creative.

### From Scrolling to Shopping
**Format:** EDUCATIONAL SERIES · CAROUSEL
**Hero:** A storefront inside the scroll.
**Card copy:** An eight-frame explainer that connects social discovery, shopping behavior and the role of dependable delivery.
**Project description:** The series maps a new consumer journey: from discovering products through short-form content and creators to the delivery experience that follows checkout. It moves from defining social commerce to the practical elements of a repeatable customer experience.
**Visual approach:** Rather than using a single promotional headline, the layouts divide a complex topic into sequential questions, diagrams and short statements. Teal UI-inspired graphics and product imagery maintain a familiar shopping context throughout.
**Sequence:**
- **Introduce:** A phone surrounded by commerce elements positions content-led discovery as the start of the shopping journey.
- **Explain:** List cards and a side-by-side journey chart translate ideas about creators, user content and checkout into digestible visual information.
- **Connect to the brand:** The final frames link consumer enthusiasm with delivery reliability, finishing on the Shadowfax logistics ecosystem.

### Black Friday, Delivered
**Format:** SEASONAL CAMPAIGN · CAROUSEL
**Hero:** Built for the busiest shopping days.
**Card copy:** A five-frame Black Friday campaign presenting logistics capacity, speed, tracking and reliability as one connected promise.
**Project description:** The cover establishes the seasonal event, then each frame focuses on a different reason a commerce brand might need a dependable delivery network during peak demand. The storytelling is business-facing, but the visuals stay human: parcels, riders and a customer tracking a shipment.
**Visual approach:** The recurring “A network…” headline structure makes the campaign work as a series. Each frame pairs one sharply phrased message with a recognizable delivery situation, leaving the product value immediately legible even during a fast scroll.
**Sequence:**
- **Peak rush:** The opening benefit centers on handling heavy parcel volume without slowing down.
- **Movement and visibility:** A rider image communicates quick pickup and same-day delivery; a phone screen brings live tracking into view.
- **Close on confidence:** The final frame returns to delivery staff and network infrastructure to underline reliability on high-demand days.

### Hidden PIN Codes: Sanganer
**Format:** STORYTELLING SERIES · CAROUSEL
**Hero:** Local craft. A much wider audience.
**Card copy:** A seven-frame visual story following Sanganer block printing from regional craft to social-commerce discovery and wider reach.
**Project description:** The story begins with Sanganer, Jaipur, and its hand-block printing tradition. It then follows the wider opportunity: customers drawn to handmade textiles, makers reaching audiences through social platforms and demand traveling beyond the craft’s place of origin.
**Visual approach:** The artwork pairs craft-focused photography and product imagery with airy composition, editorial headlines and Shadowfax’s green-and-yellow brand details. The result gives the artisans and textiles the visual space while the copy explains the commercial connection.
**Sequence:**
- **Place first:** The opening frame names Sanganer and grounds the story in its hand-block printing heritage.
- **Show the people:** Subsequent frames use artisans, textiles and creator-style photography to connect making with discovery.
- **Broaden the lens:** The final frames move toward wider commerce opportunities while keeping the craft and its makers visible.

### Inside Shadowfax
**Format:** BRAND STORY · CAROUSEL
**Hero:** Four frames. One logistics story.
**Card copy:** A concise brand-introduction carousel that moves from company scale to everyday deliveries and its decade-long story.
**Project description:** The series answers four practical questions in sequence: Who is Shadowfax? What does it do? Why do brands choose it? What does ten years of operations represent? Logistics is made tangible through vehicles, delivery partners and a map of India.
**Visual approach:** A clean white-and-green layout creates consistency while the photography changes the scale of the story: network infrastructure, doorstep delivery, a rider in motion and India-wide presence.
**Sequence:**
- **Scale:** The cover introduces reach and operational scale with a freight vehicle, aircraft and packages.
- **People:** A delivery handoff and rider image shift the focus from infrastructure to the customer-facing experience.
- **Continuity:** The final India map and anniversary message present the company’s decade milestone as the close to the introduction.

### Q1 FY27: Quarterly Highlights
**Format:** BUSINESS COMMUNICATION · DECK
**Hero:** Turning a quarter into a clear visual narrative.
**Card copy:** An eight-slide branded quarterly-highlights deck organizing operational, revenue and profitability information for quick reading.
**Project description:** The deck begins with the Q1 FY27 headline, then progresses through an executive summary, KPI dashboards, business-line comparisons and profitability charts. The challenge visible in the material is one of information density: many figures, time periods and comparison points must be read without losing the overarching narrative.
**Visual approach:** A landscape format, restrained brand palette, consistent headings and repeated chart patterns organize numerical information into a hierarchy. The pages move between high-level takeaways and detailed breakdowns rather than treating every figure as equally important.
**Sequence:**
- **Set the context:** An opening slide and summary page frame the quarter before introducing the detailed numbers.
- **Create hierarchy:** Revenue, order volume and business-line information are grouped into repeatable KPI panels, charts and labeled tables.
- **Close with clarity:** Profitability charts and the vision/mission slide provide a conclusion to the data-heavy sequence.

### Delivery Has Many Legends
**Format:** FESTIVE FILM · MOTION · 00:30
**Hero:** A festive race to the doorstep.
**Card copy:** A 30-second Christmas-themed brand film bringing a delivery rider and Santa into the same fast-moving story.
**Project description:** The film begins with a familiar Christmas delivery scene: Santa and his reindeer flying into the city. A Shadowfax rider joins the action, leading into a playful race and a doorstep delivery moment that resolves the joke around speed.

**Sequence:**
- **Setup:** Santa’s sleigh establishes the seasonal reference in the first seconds.

### Growth Shows Up
**Format:** GROWTH ANNOUNCEMENT · MOTION · 00:21
**Hero:** Make a network milestone move.
**Card copy:** A vertical announcement film using a digital truck and animated numbers to visualise expansion in PIN-code reach.
**Project description:** A point-cloud-style truck appears against a dark field. The video shifts into high-contrast numeric statements and brand-green typography, announcing a move from the earlier figure shown in the creative to 15,656 PIN codes.
**Visual approach:** The contrast between a detailed wireframe-like vehicle and spare typography makes the milestone the main event. The number is treated as the motion-led hero, not a footnote.
**Sequence:**
- **Build anticipation:** The glowing truck establishes logistics as the visual anchor.
- **Reveal the milestone:** The animation transitions into a large PIN-code count on a green field.
- **Brand close:** The closing copy connects the announced reach with businesses expanding into new geographies.

### Reach, Frame by Frame
**Format:** NETWORK REACH · MOTION · 00:15
**Hero:** A number that lands like a delivery.
**Card copy:** A 15-second vertical reach announcement built around a branded truck, moving parcels and a clear numeric reveal.
**Project description:** The film opens behind a Shadowfax truck as its doors swing open and parcels spill toward the camera. An animated location marker and PIN-code numbers then transform the delivery scene into a direct statement about the network’s reach.
**Visual approach:** One visual metaphor does the work: the truck physically opens up to reveal reach. Strong dark-to-bright contrast and a rapid sequence are designed to make the numbers readable within a short vertical format.
**Sequence:**
- **Set the scene:** A branded truck and nighttime lighting establish a recognizable logistics environment.
- **Build movement:** Opening doors and tumbling parcels create an immediate change of pace.
- **Show reach:** The video reveals its PIN-code figures and resolves on the Shadowfax mark.

### The Silence After Checkout
**Format:** EXPLAINER · VERTICAL VIDEO · 00:31
**Hero:** The sale is not the end of the story.
**Card copy:** A presenter-led vertical video explaining why delivery communication matters after a customer checks out.
**Project description:** A direct-to-camera speaker explains the cost of uncertainty between an online purchase and the parcel reaching the customer. The edit adds emphasis with captions, a short on-screen list and cutaway delivery imagery.
**Visual approach:** The visual treatment prioritizes spoken clarity: captions follow the presenter’s words, information appears briefly as supporting graphics, and a delivery cutaway gives the abstract discussion a concrete example.
**Sequence:**
- **Hook:** A direct-to-camera opening frames the post-purchase experience as the topic.
- **Evidence within the narrative:** On-screen prompts name abandoned carts, cancellations and missed repeat purchases as risks discussed in the film; they are presented as its message, not independently measured results.
- **Resolution:** The ending shifts to a doorstep delivery scene and the Shadowfax sign-off.

### When dimensions change the bill
**Archive caption:** A presenter-led explainer turns an everyday packing scenario into a clear demonstration of why parcel dimensions can affect shipping charges.
**Intro:** What looks like a 200-gram shipment can be billed differently once packaging dimensions enter the calculation. This vertical reel makes the distinction visible through a simple box demonstration before introducing Shadowfax360 as the product response.
**Creative problem visible in the film:** Translate a technical, easily misunderstood cost issue into a shipping story that a small business can follow within the opening moments of a reel.
**Story:** A presenter compares two parcel boxes: the smaller, heavier parcel and a larger, lighter one. On-screen “200gm” and “500gm” labels make the mismatch concrete. The film then cuts to parcel-weighing and warehouse imagery, using actual-weight and volumetric-weight graphics to explain why the bill can change. Shadowfax360 appears as the branded product framing for more predictable shipping costs.
**Visual treatment:** The opening relies on a person, two physical boxes and a very short numerical comparison; those elements remain legible in a vertical feed. Captions follow the spoken explanation, while weight labels, rupee symbols and product-green interstitials organize the more technical section. The longer runtime allows the video to move from the everyday example to product details without overloading its first few seconds.
**Reported metrics:** Hook rate 29%; hold rate 8.11%; plays 1.28 lakh. (Source: accompanying uploaded text file; reporting window and metric definitions not provided.)

### The cost you pay on every shipment
**Archive caption:** A fast, overhead-to-eye-level presenter reel reframes routine shipping fees as a recurring drain on merchant margins.
**Intro:** “Your biggest expense is not ads. It’s shipping.” This quick reel redirects attention to the cost attached to every fulfilled order, then introduces Shadowfax360’s advertised starting rate as a margin-conscious product proposition.
**Creative problem visible in the film:** Turn an ongoing operating cost into an immediately recognizable problem for sellers without needing a long explanation or a dense financial chart.
**Story:** An overhead camera tracks the presenter as he walks into frame and contrasts advertising with the cost of shipping. A fast transition shifts to an eye-level explanation of how repeated shipment charges affect margins. The film then presents Shadowfax360’s advertised starting rate and ends with a parcel handoff and branded sign-off.
**Visual treatment:** The overhead entrance creates a different first-frame silhouette from a conventional talking-head reel. Tracking movement, short caption blocks and a quick shift in camera angle create pacing. A bright price callout and an on-camera package moment return the abstract margin discussion to a physical delivery.
